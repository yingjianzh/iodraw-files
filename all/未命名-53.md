```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([获取Google Drive备份]) --> CheckAuth[检查Google Drive授权]
    CheckAuth --> AuthStatus{已授权?}
    
    AuthStatus -->|否| InitiateAuth[启动OAuth授权流程]
    AuthStatus -->|是| SearchFiles[搜索备份文件]
    
    InitiateAuth --> RedirectToGoogle[重定向到Google认证页面]
    RedirectToGoogle --> UserGrants[用户授予权限]
    UserGrants --> GetAuthToken[获取授权令牌]
    GetAuthToken --> SearchFiles
    
    SearchFiles --> QueryDrive[查询名称匹配"backup-*.zip"的文件]
    QueryDrive --> ParseResults[解析查询结果]
    ParseResults --> SortBackups[按时间戳排序备份]
    
    SortBackups --> BuildBackupList[构建备份列表显示]
    BuildBackupList --> DisplayToUser[显示列表给用户]
    DisplayToUser --> UserSelects[用户选择备份]
    
    UserSelects --> GetFileID[获取所选备份的文件ID]
    GetFileID --> End([返回文件ID])
```
