```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([开始数据获取]) --> FetchTables[获取表列表]
    FetchTables --> ProcessNextTable[处理下一个表]
    
    ProcessNextTable --> TableLeft{还有表?}
    TableLeft -->|否| CompleteProcess[完成数据获取]
    TableLeft -->|是| EstimateTableSize[估计表大小]
    
    EstimateTableSize --> SizeCheck{表大小检查}
    SizeCheck -->|< 500KB| FetchWholeTable[获取整表数据]
    SizeCheck -->|≥ 500KB| PrepareChunking[准备分块获取]
    
    FetchWholeTable --> CompressTable[压缩表数据]
    CompressTable --> EncryptTable[加密表数据]
    EncryptTable --> SaveTableFile[保存表文件]
    SaveTableFile --> UpdateManifest[更新清单]
    UpdateManifest --> UpdateProgress[更新进度(表级)]
    UpdateProgress --> ProcessNextTable
    
    PrepareChunking --> CalculateChunks[计算分块策略]
    CalculateChunks --> ProcessNextChunk[处理下一个块]
    
    ProcessNextChunk --> ChunkLeft{还有块?}
    ChunkLeft -->|否| TableComplete[表处理完成]
    ChunkLeft -->|是| FetchChunkData[获取块数据]
    
    FetchChunkData --> GetChunkRecords{记录获取方式}
    GetChunkRecords -->|ID范围| FetchByRange[按ID范围获取]
    GetChunkRecords -->|分页| FetchByOffset[按偏移量获取]
    
    FetchByRange --> SerializeChunk[序列化块数据]
    FetchByOffset --> SerializeChunk
    
    SerializeChunk --> CompressChunk[压缩块数据]
    CompressChunk --> EncryptChunk[加密块数据]
    EncryptChunk --> SaveChunkFile[保存块文件]
    SaveChunkFile --> UpdateChunkInfo[更新块信息]
    UpdateChunkInfo --> UpdateProgress2[更新进度(块级)]
    UpdateProgress2 --> ProcessNextChunk
    
    TableComplete --> UpdateProgress
    CompleteProcess --> CreateChecksums[创建校验和文件]
    CreateChecksums --> End([结束数据获取])
```
