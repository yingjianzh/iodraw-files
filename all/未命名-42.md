```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start[开始计算内容哈希] --> ExcludeFields[排除系统字段\nid/contentHash/lastModified]
    ExcludeFields --> SortFields[对字段名进行排序]
    SortFields --> ProcessData[处理字段内容]
    
    ProcessData --> SimpleValues[处理简单值类型]
    ProcessData --> Objects[处理对象类型]
    ProcessData --> Arrays[处理数组类型]
    
    SimpleValues --> Serialize[序列化为规范JSON]
    Objects --> DeepSort[深度排序对象键]
    Arrays --> PreserveOrder[保持数组顺序]
    
    DeepSort --> Serialize
    PreserveOrder --> Serialize
    
    Serialize --> ComputeHash[计算字符串哈希]
    ComputeHash --> End[返回哈希值]
```
