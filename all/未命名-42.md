```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start[开始计算表级哈希] --> QueryRecords[查询组件表的所有记录]
    QueryRecords --> ExtractHashes[提取每条记录的contentHash字段]
    
    ExtractHashes --> CreateHashMap[创建记录ID与哈希的映射]
    CreateHashMap --> SortByIds[按记录ID排序]
    
    SortByIds --> ConcatHashes[拼接所有哈希值\n格式: id1:hash1|id2:hash2|...]
    ConcatHashes --> ComputeTableHash[计算拼接字符串的哈希值]
    
    ComputeTableHash --> StoreResult[存储表级哈希结果]
    StoreResult --> End[完成]
```
