```mermaid
flowchart TD
    %% 主要分支
    Start([开始]) --> UserAction{用户操作}
    UserAction -->|模式切换请求\n本地→在线| TryLock[尝试获取用户数据锁]
    UserAction -->|其他操作| OtherOps[处理其他操作]
    
    %% 定时删除任务分支
    ScheduledTask([定时删除任务启动]) --> QueryDeletions[查询t_data_deletion获取待删除数据]
    QueryDeletions --> HasTasks{有待删除数据?}
    HasTasks -->|否| TaskEnd([定时任务结束])
    HasTasks -->|是| GroupByUser[按用户分组]
    
    %% 处理用户数据删除
    GroupByUser --> ProcessNextUser[处理下一个用户]
    ProcessNextUser --> AcquireLock[获取该用户数据锁]
    AcquireLock --> LockSuccess{获取成功?}
    
    LockSuccess -->|否| SkipUser[跳过该用户]
    SkipUser --> MoreUsers1{还有其他用户?}
    MoreUsers1 -->|是| ProcessNextUser
    MoreUsers1 -->|否| TaskEnd
    
    LockSuccess -->|是| GetData[根据组件id/组件id+组件实例id查询t_component_table_hash表,获取对应组件待删除的数据表]
    
    GetData --> UpdateData[更新t_data_deletion,t_component_table_hash对应数据状态为progress]
    UpdateData --> DeleteData[按照组件表分批执行数据删除]
    DeleteData --> DeleteCompleted[删除完成]
    DeleteCompleted --> UpdateDataDeletion[]
    DeleteCompleted --> ReleaseLock[释放用户数据锁]
    ReleaseLock --> MoreUsers2{还有其他用户?}
    MoreUsers2 -->|是| ProcessNextUser
    MoreUsers2 -->|否| TaskEnd
    
    %% 模式切换流程
    TryLock --> LockResult{获取锁结果}
    
    LockResult -->|失败| ReturnWaiting[返回等待状态]
    ReturnWaiting --> SetupPolling[设置客户端轮询]
    SetupPolling --> End1([结束])
    
    LockResult -->|成功| CheckPendingDeletion{查询t_data_deletion\n有待删除数据?}
    
    CheckPendingDeletion -->|有| RemoveDeletionRecords[删除该用户所有\n待删除记录]
    RemoveDeletionRecords --> PerformSync[执行数据同步]
    
    CheckPendingDeletion -->|无| PerformSync
    
    PerformSync --> ReleaseSwitchLock[释放用户数据锁]
    ReleaseSwitchLock --> ReturnComplete[返回完成状态]
    ReturnComplete --> End2([结束])
    
    %% 客户端轮询
    ClientPolling[客户端开始轮询] --> PollLockStatus[轮询锁状态]
    PollLockStatus --> StillLocked{锁仍存在?}
    
    StillLocked -->|是| WaitInterval[等待一段时间]
    WaitInterval --> PollLockStatus
    
    StillLocked -->|否| StartSwitch[开始模式切换]
    StartSwitch --> CompleteUI[更新UI为完成]
    CompleteUI --> End3([结束])
```
